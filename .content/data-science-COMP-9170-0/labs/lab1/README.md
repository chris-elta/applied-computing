# Lab 1: Exploratory Data Analysis and Descriptive Statistics

Notebook: `lab1.ipynb` (all six tasks: questions, cleaning, descriptive statistics, density/ECDF, sampling variability, Bayes' theorem, plus a reflection). It was run top to bottom in a fresh kernel (execution counts 1-19) with no errors.

## Findings in plain English

- **Baseball (annual BA, 154 years, 1871-2024, equal weight per year).** Typical annual BA is about 0.262 (median 0.260, SD about 0.013). The distribution is modestly right-skewed (skewness about 0.71); the mean is only about 0.002 above the median. Years before 1950 had a higher average BA (about 0.267) than 1950 onward (about 0.257). Only 1894 (0.309) and 1895 (0.296) are flagged as high outliers by the 1.5 x IQR rule; they were kept, and dropping them barely changes the results (mean 0.2621 vs 0.2615). The mean of annual values is _not_ a pooled player batting average.
- **NYC green taxi fares (9,949 trips after cleaning).** The mean fare is $18.46 but the median is only $14.20, because a small share of long trips pull the mean up. About 73% of trips cost $20 or less. 743 fares (7.5%) are flagged as high outliers by the 1.5 x IQR rule; their median distance is 9.5 miles versus 1.9 for all trips, so most look like genuine long trips and were kept.
- **Sampling experiment.** Means of samples of 30 vary about 2.5 times less (BA) and 2.6 times less (fares) in SD than means of samples of 5, close to the sqrt(6) = 2.4 expected from the 1/n rule. The simulated variances agree approximately with (population variance) / n: ratios 1.07 and 1.03 for BA, 0.97 and 0.88 for fares (n = 5 and n = 30). The agreement is looser for the heavy-tailed fares.
- **Bayes (A = Year >= 1950, B = BA >= 0.260).** P(A) = 75/154, P(B|A) = 28/75, P(B|not A) = 51/79, P(B) = 79/154, so P(A|B) = 28/79 = 0.354, confirmed by direct counting. A high-BA year is more likely to be pre-1950 (0.646). This is an in-sample description, not a causal claim or a measured predictive accuracy.

Limitations: results about baseball describe annual league aggregates only (nothing about players); taxi results describe 2024 green-taxi trips with a positive metered fare and valid timestamps (not other years, other vehicles, or trends over time). The simulation builds in independence, which real years and real trips do not have.

## Cleaning and sampling choices (taxi)

1. Read every column as text; convert numeric columns explicitly (thousands separators are stripped first; no value failed conversion).
2. Keep trips with a 2024 pickup date (660,218 -> 660,198 rows).
3. Draw 10,000 rows without replacement (`random_state=42`); save as `taxi_sample.csv`.
4. Exclude 10 trips with dropoff not after pickup and 41 with fare <= 0 (12 zero, 29 negative; mostly no-charge or disputed payment types). Removed rows are **not** replaced. Final size: **9,949**.
5. No fully identical rows were found. Zero-distance trips (552), very long or costly trips and outlier fares were kept and documented.
