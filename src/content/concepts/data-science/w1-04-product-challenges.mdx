The breakout asks a group to name one likely challenge in each of six areas when the team
turns the music research into a product:

| Area | Sample question from the slides |
| --- | --- |
| Machine learning | What does correctness or accuracy really mean? Which accuracy do customers care about? |
| Engineering | Can we cope with an influx of customers? Generation takes longer as customers grow — what can we do? |
| Operating and updating | Models improve continuously. When do we deploy? Can we roll back? |
| Team or management | None given — the slides leave this one for your group to supply |
| Business | Buy more machines or move to the cloud? |
| Safety or ethics | What are unacceptable mistakes? Might we leak private data? |

## The questions the slides raise

The discussion slides do not answer these. Read them as a checklist of things a research
paper never had to consider.

**Knowing how you are doing.** How can we see how well we are doing in practice? Can we
estimate how good the generated music is, and how do we do for different customers or
genres? Will the same prompt give the same result twice, and does it matter?

**When it breaks.** Generation sometimes crashes. How do we achieve high availability? How
do we see that all is fine, and page somebody if not? What happens when Tensorflow updates —
does our infrastructure still work?

**When it succeeds.** With more customers, generation slows down. How do we handle large
amounts of data per day? Can we offer live generation as an app or web service?

**What we are allowed to do.** Can and should we learn from customer data? How do we debug
problem files we are not allowed to see? Can competitors or bad actors attack the system?

## The pattern

Almost none of these questions is about the model. They concern measurement, reliability,
change and responsibility — the rest of the system the model lives inside.

> The model is one component. Most of the product is everything else.
