The lecture says the critical issue in any modelling project is **finding the right data
set**, and adds that this will certainly be the case for your projects. A good model on the
wrong data answers the wrong question.

## Metadata

Large data sets often arrive with valuable **metadata**: book titles, image captions,
Wikipedia edit history. That information was recorded for its own purposes, but it can be
turned to others. A caption written to describe a photo is also a human label for what the
photo contains; an edit history is a record of which topics people argue about.

The lecture's phrase is that **repurposing metadata requires imagination.** Ask what else the
labels around the data could tell you.
